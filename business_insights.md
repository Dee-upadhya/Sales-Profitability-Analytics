# Business Insights

This analysis is designed to demonstrate the complete analyst workflow: **question → data → analysis → insight → action**.

> The dataset is synthetic and created for portfolio purposes. Numerical findings below are generated from this dataset and should not be presented as real company results.

## Questions answered

1. Is revenue growth translating into profit growth?
2. Which products and regions drive revenue?
3. Which high-revenue areas have weaker margins?
4. How does discounting relate to gross margin?
5. Where should management investigate first?

## Insight framework

- **What happened?** Describe the KPI movement.
- **Why did it happen?** Break the movement down by product, region, channel or segment.
- **Why does it matter?** Connect the finding to revenue/profitability.
- **What could be investigated next?** Turn the insight into a business action.

## Suggested actions

- Review discounting on low-margin products.
- Monitor revenue growth together with gross-margin growth.
- Investigate regional/channel combinations with strong sales but weak margins.
- Identify high-margin products that could support profitable growth.
- Build monthly KPI monitoring with thresholds for management review.
